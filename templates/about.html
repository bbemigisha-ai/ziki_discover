About Ziki Discover

Ziki Discover is a music discovery platform dedicated to celebrating African music by helping users explore artists, songs and albums from across the continent. Through powerful music APIs, users can discover new music, listen to previews and learn more about the artists shaping Africa's vibrant music industry.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make African music easier to discover by bringing together artists from different countries and genres into one simple, interactive and engaging platform. Ziki Discover aims to showcase Africa's musical diversity while encouraging users to explore new sounds beyond what they already know.

Key Features

Search Music

Search for African artists, songs and albums instantly using the Deezer API.

Browse by Country

Explore artists representing countries across Africa including Uganda, Rwanda, Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, Tanzania and South Africa.

Browse by Genre

Discover music through popular African genres such as Afrobeats, Amapiano, Gospel, Highlife, Hip Hop and Bongo Flava.

Song Preview

Listen to 30-second previews before exploring an artist's profile for more information.

Technologies & APIs

Deezer API

Provides artist information, songs, album artwork and music previews used throughout the platform.

MusicBrainz API

Provides artist metadata including country of origin, helping users learn more about African musicians.

Flask

Powers the backend of the application, handles routing and integrates data from multiple APIs.

HTML, CSS & JavaScript

Used to create a responsive, interactive and visually engaging user experience.

Why African Music?

African music has become one of the world's fastest-growing creative industries, with genres such as Afrobeats, Amapiano and Bongo Flava gaining international recognition. Despite this growth, discovering artists from different African countries can still be challenging. Ziki Discover was created to bridge this gap by providing a single platform where users can explore the richness, diversity and cultural heritage of African music.
